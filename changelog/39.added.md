`ConnectivityIntent` and `InfrastructureBinding` Infrahub schemas — the middle two objects of the business intent chain (#184). `ConnectivityIntent.status` (requested/approved/active/decommissioned) is what makes orphaned-rule detection possible: a rule still deployed under a decommissioned intent is the audit violation the platform exists to surface. `InfrastructureBinding` separates what was asked for from how it was realised, and carries the device link that is the first hop of any reverse-audit walk.
